## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Carousel slides SHALL match the platform

`carousel render` SHALL size slides for the chosen platform: 1080×1350 or 1080×1440 for Instagram, 1080×1920 for TikTok.

#### Scenario: TikTok

- **WHEN** a carousel is rendered for TikTok
- **THEN** every slide is 1080×1920
