# Carousels

## Why

Carousels are the other daily format. A carousel uses its post's script and images.

## What Changes

- `carousel render --platform instagram|tiktok` makes 5–10 slides with text over images. Sizes: Instagram 4:5 (1080×1350) or 3:4 (1080×1440), TikTok photo mode 9:16 (1080×1920). Each slide uses a chosen crop of its image.

Not part of this change: scheduling and publishing.
