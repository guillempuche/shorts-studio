# Carousels

## Why

Carousels are the other daily format, and they share the script and images of a post.

## What Changes

- `carousel render --platform instagram|tiktok`: 5–10 slides with text over images; Instagram 4:5 (1080×1350) or 3:4 (1080×1440), TikTok photo mode 9:16 (1080×1920), with an explicit crop from each image.

Out of scope: scheduling and publishing.
