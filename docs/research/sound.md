# Sound and rhythm

Cutting to music, voice speed, pauses, and mixing. See [README.md](README.md) for the summary and the evidence levels. Most of this will apply once voice and music generation arrive ([voice-music-animate](../../openspec/changes/voice-music-animate/proposal.md)).

## Rules

| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| People remember images shown on a regular musical beat better than images shown off it.                                                                                      | Moderate (lab)           | [Johndro et al. 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0001691819300149)                                                                                         |
| People notice sound that runs ahead of the picture from about 45 ms, and sound behind it from about 125 ms. | Faster tempo raises excitement. Major keys sound positive and minor keys negative. People like fast music in a major key and slow music in a minor key more than mismatches. | Strong                   | [Hunter & Schellenberg review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3726864/)                                                                                                      |

**Muted viewing** figures conflict: 85% muted (Facebook feed, 2016, publisher-reported), 69% muted in public (Verizon/Publicis survey, 2019), 80% of Reels with sound on (Instagram, 2022). Captions stay on for accessibility and for noisy places. But the data doesn't support "most people watch muted" for Reels or TikTok today.

## What shorts-studio will do with it

With music, beats cut every 2, 4 or 8 musical beats, so each image lasts about 2–2.5 s. Cuts snap to downbeats and are never late. Section changes and drops always get a cut. With speech over music, cuts keep to word boundaries and snap to a beat only within ±120 ms. Voices default to about 160 words per minute: faster on hooks, slower on reflective lines. Music sits 12 LU under the voice, and the mix targets −14 LUFS. The numbers will live in [`packages/domain/src/direction.ts`](../../packages/domain/src/direction.ts).

## Open questions

- No study shows beat-synced cuts raise retention or shares on the platforms.
- There's no data on how many beats to use per cut, or on whether syncopation helps.
- Nobody publishes how tempos are spread across trending audio.
