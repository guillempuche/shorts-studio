TikTok's ad advice is "5–10 words per second" of on-screen text. That contradicts every reading standard, so shorts-studio ignores it.

## What shorts-studio does with it

- A caption groups at most 4 words, about 42 characters (it fits on two lines of up to 25) and 17 characters per second. It stays up at least 0.8 s by running into the pause that follows. A word never stands alone, except at the very end.
- Captions sit centred around y≈1100. That keeps them clear of every platform's bottom zone and of the buttons on the right.
- Highlighting each word is an option (`captions.highlight` in config.json). It's off by default.
- An optional hook title of 3–7 words shows in the upper safe band for the first few seconds (`script set --hook`).

The numbers are in [`packages/domain/src/direction.ts`](../../packages/domain/src/direction.ts).

## Open questions

- No public A/B data compares highlighted and plain captions for retention in short videos.
- Safe-zone pixels come from third parties reading the templates. They change when the apps update.
