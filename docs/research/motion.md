# Camera and image motion

How each still image should move. Summary and evidence levels are explained in [README.md](README.md). No controlled study gives speeds or zoom amounts for Ken Burns-style motion: the studies back the direction of each effect, the numbers come from practice.

## Rules

| Rule                                                                                                                                                              | Evidence                                 | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Motion raises arousal more than it changes how pleasant something feels; the content sets the mood, motion sets energy and attention.                             | Moderate                                 | [Detenber et al. 1998](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/08838159809364437); [Yilmaz et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10352452/)                                        |
| Approaching (looming) images make unpleasant content stronger and more arousing; push-ins suit tension.                                                           | Moderate (lab, still images)             | [Mühlberger et al. 2008](https://doi.apa.org/doi/10.1037/1528-3542.8.2.192)                                                                                                                            |
| A zoom reads as attention ("look here"), a dolly as moving closer; a scale zoom on a flat still is a zoom. Parallax or a video clip gives the "entering" feeling. | Moderate                                 | [Heimann et al. 2019](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0211026)                                                                                                       |
| Constant movement wears out; holding still on some beats gives motion on others its meaning.                                                                      | Critic, practitioner                     | [Bordwell, Calm that camera](https://www.davidbordwell.net/blog/2023/04/30/calm-that-camera/)                                                                                                          |
| Push-in is emphasis and realisation; pull-back reveals context or isolates a figure.                                                                              | Strong as convention                     | [Bordwell & Thompson, Film Art ch. 5](https://files.commons.gc.cuny.edu/wp-content/blogs.dir/8374/files/2019/08/David-Bordwell-Kristin-Thompson-Jeff-Smith-Film-Art_-An-Introduction-Chapter-5-ok.pdf) |
| Ken Burns practice: the camera explores a photo like an eye, moving to a meaningful detail.                                                                       | Practitioner                             | [NEH on Ken Burns](https://www.neh.gov/humanities/2015/septemberoctober/feature/the-civil-war-ken-burns-reinvented-the-television-history-d)                                                           |
| Left-to-right movement reads as more positive for left-to-right readers; it reverses for right-to-left readers.                                                   | Moderate, cultural                       | [Egizii et al. 2017](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312217873)                                                                                                                               |
| Up is associated with good, down with bad; untested on camera motion.                                                                                             | Strong for the metaphor, weak for motion | [Meier & Robinson 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15043641/)                                                                                                                                     |
| Cuts are least noticed when motion continues across them.                                                                                                         | Strong                                   | [Smith 2012](https://eprints.bbk.ac.uk/6679/1/6679.pdf)                                                                                                                                                |
| Scaling, zooming and parallax can trigger motion sickness; shake makes things feel anxious and immediate.                                                         | Moderate                                 | [WebKit on motion](https://webkit.org/blog/7551/responsive-design-for-motion/)                                                                                                                         |

## What shorts-studio does with it

Each beat gets a motion type from its story moment, which the agent can override: `hold`, `push-slow`, `push-punch`, `pull-back`, `pan-right`, `pan-left`, `drift-up`, `drift-down`. Speeds fall in bands (calm about 1–2.5 % per second, neutral 3–4, punch 8 or more), total zoom stays at 15% or less, and images are generated about 25% larger than the frame so motion never needs upscaling past the image. Cuts are hard cuts. Numbers are in [`src/domain/direction.ts`](../../src/domain/direction.ts).

## Open questions

- None of these studies used phones, 9:16 frames or 2-second shots.
- Direction effects (left/right, up/down) are small and may be swamped by the image.
- The best speed has no source: worth testing retention across the bands.
